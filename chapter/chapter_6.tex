\chapter{Results and Discussion}

This section presents the observable results of the implemented system through the user interface (UI) and discusses how each UI component supports the overall workflow of site selection and location analysis. The key outcome of the project is a single, map-driven interface that allows users to (i) start a new project from a workspace, (ii) input business requirements through an intake wizard, and (iii) proceed to a results view that summarizes the location characteristics across multiple analytical dimensions for decision-making.

\section{Workspace and Project Initialization}

Figure 6.1 shows the \textit{Home/Workspace} page of the application, titled Discover the Perfect Location. The design acts as a central hub for the user:

  \caption{Home and Workspace page. Users can initiate a new suitability analysis or access their recent projects.}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Suitability Analysis:} A clear call-to-action allowing the user to start a new comprehensive site analysis.
    \item \textbf{Recent Projects:} A list of previously conducted analyses (e.g., Kathmandu Central Cafe Analysis, Lalitpur Premium Branch), providing quick access to past results and scores.
\end{itemize}


\section{Intake Wizard}

Once a new analysis is initiated, the user is presented with the \textit{Intake Wizard} (Figure 6.2). This step ensures the analysis is tailored to the specific business persona:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Project Identity and Type:} Collects the project name, brand name, and the specific type of café (e.g., Specialty Coffee, Fast-Food).
    \item \textbf{Business Stage and Audience:} Captures the stage (New, Expanding, Relocating) and target demographic.
    \item \textbf{Location Selection:} The user selects the target city and a center point on the interactive map for the analysis.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{img/intake_wizard.jpeg}
  \caption{Intake Wizard. The left panel collects detailed business requirements, while the map allows the user to pinpoint the target location.}
  \label{fig:intake-wizard}
\end{figure}

\section{Analysis Results and Insights}

After the intake phase, the system computes the suitability of the selected location using the underlying GNN model and presents a series of detailed analytical views.

\subsection{Analysis Overview}
Figure 6.3 shows the \textit{Analysis Overview} (Business Fit Report). This is the primary dashboard for interpreting the location's viability:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Score Overview:} Presents the raw GNN Location Score alongside a context-adjusted Business Fit score.
    \item \textbf{Score Breakdown:} Deconstructs the score into contributing factors such as Location (GNN), POI Richness, Audience Fit, and Road Accessibility.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{img/analysis_overview.jpeg}
  \caption{Analysis Overview. Displays the Business Fit Report with overall scores and risk assessment based on the model's predictions.}
  \label{fig:analysis-overview}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Nearby POIs}
Figure 6.4 illustrates the \textit{Nearby POIs} view, which provides context on the surrounding urban amenities.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Amenities by Category:} Lists places of interest such as Restaurants, Cafes, and Banks, alongside their distance from the target location.
    \item \textbf{Coverage Metrics:} Summarizes the total number of POIs found within the defined search radius.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{img/nearby_pois.jpeg}
  \caption{Nearby POIs view. Categorizes local amenities and displays their proximity to the candidate location.}
  \label{fig:nearby-pois}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Competitor Data}
Understanding the competitive landscape is crucial. Figure 6.5 shows the \textit{Competitor Data} tab:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Competitor Density:} A breakdown of how many competing businesses exist within 0.5 km, 1 km, and 1.5 km radii.
    \item \textbf{Competitor Details:} Lists individual competitors, displaying their ratings, distance, estimated revenue tier, and relative strength in the area.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{img/competitor_data.jpeg}
  \caption{Competitor Data view. Provides an analysis of market saturation and details of nearby competing businesses.}
  \label{fig:competitor-data}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Foot Traffic Analysis}
The \textit{Foot Traffic Analysis} (Figure 6.6) provides temporal patterns to help assess the location's liveliness at different times:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Activity Patterns:} Bar charts visualize hourly activity and weekly patterns, indicating the busiest times and days.
    \item \textbf{Peak Periods:} Identifies the percentage of traffic during Morning Rush, Lunch Peak, and Evening Social hours.
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\linewidth]{img/foot_traffic.jpeg}
  \caption{Foot Traffic Analysis view. Visualizes temporal activity trends, peak hours, and daily footfall metrics.}
  \label{fig:foot-traffic}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Area Reviews}
Finally, the \textit{Area Reviews} tab (Figure 6.7) captures the qualitative sentiment of the selected location:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Sentiment Analysis:} A progress bar format indicating the proportion of positive, neutral, and negative reviews in the area.
    \item \textbf{Top Keywords:} Highlights frequently mentioned terms (e.g., ``good coffee'', ``crowded'', ``expensive'') for quick insights.
    \item \textbf{Recent Reviews:} Displays snippets of actual reviews to provide deeper qualitative context.
\end{itemize}
