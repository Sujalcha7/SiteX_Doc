\chapter{Conclusion and Enhancements}
\section*{Conclusion}

In conclusion, SiteX has been successfully developed as a Location Intelligence and Site-Suitability application tailored specifically for cafe and restaurant businesses. The system enables entrepreneurs to evaluate potential locations by selecting a point on the map and receiving a suitability score along with an associated risk level. 

A major achievement of the 8th semester was the successful transition from a foundational XGBoost baseline to an advanced Heterogeneous Graph Neural Network (HeteroGNN). By modeling surrounding commercial activity, competitor presence, and neighborhood characteristics as an interconnected graph, the platform effectively captures complex, multi-hop spatial dependencies. SiteX thus bridges the gap between raw geospatial data and real-world business decisions, helping reduce uncertainty and financial risk when launching or expanding a food-service business in Nepal.

\section*{Future Enhancements}

While the current system provides a strong foundation for data-driven site evaluation, several enhancements can further improve its accuracy, usability, and scalability:

    \begin{itemize}

            \item \textbf{Real-time live traffic data integration (via mobile APIs):} Incorporate live mobility and traffic feeds from mobile location APIs to provide dynamic, up-to-date location assessments.
            \item \textbf{Integration of macro-economic indicators (real estate pricing APIs):} Incorporate broader pricing and economic signals to improve long-term site viability modeling.
            \item \textbf{Multi-city scaling beyond the Kathmandu Valley:} Extend the platform to additional urban regions so the same workflow can support entrepreneurs in multiple markets.
    \end{itemize}
